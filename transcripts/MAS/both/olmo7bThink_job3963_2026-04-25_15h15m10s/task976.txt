Question:
Which of the following is classified as a conjugate acid-base pair?

Correct Answer: H3O+ / H2O

A pair where one is the acid and the other is its conjugate base, or one is the base and the other is its conjugate acid, qualifies as a conjugate acid-base pair. Ammonia and water form a conjugate acid-base pair in the Brønsted-Lowry theory. The Brønsted-Lowry theory defines acids as proton donors and bases as proton acceptors. Therefore, ammonia and water are a conjugate acid-base pair
