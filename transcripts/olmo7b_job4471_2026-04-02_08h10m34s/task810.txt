Question:
Monoclonal antisera are distinguished from polyclonal antisera in which of the following ways?

Correct Answer: A monoclonal antiserum contains antibodies secreted from the descendants of a single B lymphocyte; a polyclonal antiserum contains antibodies secreted from the descendants of different B lymphocytes.
