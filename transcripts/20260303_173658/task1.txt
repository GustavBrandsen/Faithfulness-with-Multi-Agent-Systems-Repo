Task Name: evacuation_north_hill

Description:
You are part of a community leadership team tasked with deciding the safest evacuation route for your village during a natural disaster. Your village is located near a mountain range and a river, and you have received warnings about potential hazards. You have three options for evacuation: 

- West City: Accessible via a bridge over the river, but the bridge is currently under construction.
- East Town: Accessible through a tunnel on middle ground, but the tunnel is blocked by debris.
- North Hill: Accessible via a driveway and walking trails, but the trails are closed due to fallen trees.

You have limited time to make this decision before the situation worsens. Your task is to discuss with your fellow community leaders to determine the safest evacuation route. You will receive a reward if you choose the correct route and all other participants also choose the correct route.

After the discussion:
- If you choose the correct location, you will earn $1.
- If all other participants also choose the correct location, you will earn an additional $1 (for a total of $2).
This means that coordinating with others is crucial to maximize your rewards. The chat will at most take 15 minutes. However, the exact time when the chat will end is unknown.

Possible Answers:
  - West City
  - East Town
  - North Hill

Correct Answer: East Town

Shared Information:
  Fact 1: The local government announced that hotels in West City are prepared to accommodate evacuees. While these hotels are fully stocked with food, they may lack medical supplies.
  Fact 2: The mayor of East Town has offered accommodations for any evacuees. She also ensures that volunteers are available to assist them.
  Fact 3: The school at North Hill can serve as a temporary evacuation center, providing a two-week supply of essentials and sleeping space in the gym.
  Fact 4: The bridge to West City is currently under construction.

Hidden Information (pool):
  Hidden 1: The supply truck headed to the village from East Town was stuck in the tunnel.
  Hidden 2: The walking trails have been closed since last weekend due to fallen trees.
  Hidden 3: Several villagers reported that a mudslide just occurred, covering the driveway to North Hill.
  Hidden 4: A massive fire has blocked the supply truck and all other traffic.
