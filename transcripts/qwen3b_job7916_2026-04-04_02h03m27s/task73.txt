Using Fermat's theorem, find the remainder of 3^47 when it is divided by 23.

    Reasoning: Fermat's Little Theorem states \(a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \mod p\) for a prime \(p\) and integer \(a\) not divisible by \(p\). Here, \(p = 23\), so \(a^{22} \equiv 1 \mod 23\).

Rewrite the exponent 47 as \(47 = 22 + 25\). Thus, \(3^{47} = 3^{22+25} = (3^{22})(3^{25})\).

By Fermat's Little Theorem, \(3^{22} \equiv 1 \mod 23\). Therefore, \(3^{47} \equiv 1 \cdot 3^{25} \mod 23\).

Now, reduce the exponent 25 modulo 22: \(25 \mod 22 = 3\). So, \(3^{25} \equiv 3^3 \mod 23\).

Calculate \(3^3 = 27\). Reduce modulo 23: \(27 \mod 23 = 4\).

Thus, \(3^{47} \equiv 4 \mod 23\)
